---
name: reflect
description: Turns my feedback into a lasting rule. Use when I correct your output, say something was wrong or annoying, or say to remember something.
---
1. Write the correction as one clear, general rule that would apply to future work, not just this one output.
2. Show it to me, then add it under "## Explicit" in Meta/Preferences.md.
3. Don't duplicate or contradict an existing rule. If one conflicts, ask me which wins.
4. Add a line to OS/CHANGELOG.md: today's date — new preference — <the rule>.
5. Confirm in one line. From now on, every skill follows this rule.
