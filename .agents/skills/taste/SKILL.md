---
name: taste
description: Teaches the agent my design taste from a reference or a description. Use when I share a link, screenshot, or design I like or dislike, describe a look I want, or say "add this to my taste".
---
1. Look at what I shared: a URL, an image in Taste/references/, a pasted image, or a description. If it's a URL you can't open, ask me to describe it or add a screenshot.
2. Name what stands out in 3 to 5 bullets: colors (with hex codes if you can tell), fonts or type style, layout, mood, and tone of any text.
3. Ask me in one line: "What do you like most about this?" Use my answer to decide what matters.
4. Add an entry to the top of Taste/references.md: today's date — what it is — link or file — what I like about it.
5. Update Taste/taste.md: add or sharpen principles under the right headings. If something I dislike came up, add it under "Never". Replace any [placeholder] lines you can. Don't contradict an existing principle without asking me.
6. Add a line to OS/CHANGELOG.md: today's date — taste updated — <what changed>.
7. Show me only the lines you changed in taste.md.
