# Student pack

| Skill | Run it with | What it does | Writes to |
|---|---|---|---|
| `class-summary` | `/class-summary Inbox/lecture.txt` | Turns a lecture transcript or slides into a clean lesson note | `Academics/Courses/<course>/Lessons/` |
| `study-prep` | `/study-prep Database Systems, midterm` | Makes a reviewer, flashcards, or a practice test from your lesson notes | `Academics/Courses/<course>/Reviewers/` |
| `thesis` | `/thesis status` | Tracks thesis chapters, milestones, advisor feedback, and remaining subjects | `Academics/Thesis/`, `Academics/Subjects/` |

They build on each other: `class-summary` makes lesson notes, `study-prep` turns them into a reviewer, and `daily-open` picks up exam dates and thesis deadlines.

Use them to understand and organize your learning. Follow your school's AI policy, and don't submit AI work as your own.
