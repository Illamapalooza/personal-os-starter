---
name: study-prep
description: Makes a reviewer, flashcards, or a practice test from my lesson notes. Use when I ask to study, review, make a reviewer, or prepare for a quiz or exam.
---
1. Ask in one message, and accept short answers: Which course? Which lessons or topics (or "everything so far")? Reviewer, flashcards, or practice test? When is the exam?
2. Read the matching notes in Academics/Courses/<Course>/Lessons/. If there aren't any, say so and suggest running class-summary first.
3. Make what I asked for and save it to Academics/Courses/<Course>/Reviewers/<topic>-<type>.md:
   - **Reviewer:** each core concept with a definition, key points, and an example. End with the 3 ideas most likely to confuse me.
   - **Flashcards:** 15 question-and-answer pairs, short enough to answer from memory.
   - **Practice test:** 10 multiple choice, 3 short answer, and 1 essay question. Put the answer key at the end, with a one-line explanation for each.
4. Follow how I like things explained (OS/Context/about-me.md).
5. Base everything on my notes and link back to the source lessons. Don't add outside facts. If something seems missing, list it under "Check with your notes or instructor".
6. If the exam date isn't in Tasks.md yet, add it. Suggest one study block per day until the exam in a single line.
